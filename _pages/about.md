I am a PhD candidate at University of Twente with the https://www.utwente.nl/en/eemcs/dmb/. I am also an Assistant Lecturer in the department of Computer Engineering at Busitema University.

My PhD is supervised by Assistant Professor Estefanía Talavera (University of Twente), Dr. Godliver Owomugisha (Busitema University) and Prof. Luuk Spreeuwers (University of Twente)

## Research interests
My research sits at the intersection of computer vision, spectral imaging, and artificial intelligence for agriculture. I focus on multi-crop plant disease detection, with the goal of building accurate and affordable tools that farmers can use in the field.

#Topics I work on include:

#- Multi-spectral and spectral imaging for crop disease detection
#- Deep learning and hierarchical classification for multi-crop leaf disease recognition
#- Efficient AI: model compression (pruning and quantization) for deployment on mobile and edge devices
#- Low-cost computer vision systems for agriculture

### Current projects

#**BUAIIR-Spectra** – a multi-spectral crop disease dataset.

#**Multi-crop Leaf Disease (MLD) benchmark** – a benchmark and hierarchical classification models (HiLeaD) for recognising diseases across multiple crops.

#**Edge deployment** – compressing hierarchical disease-recognition models so they run efficiently on mobile devices.

### Teaching and supervision

I teach in the Department of Computer Engineering at Busitema University and supervise MSc students working on AI and computer vision.
